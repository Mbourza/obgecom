## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### General Questions
**Q: Is OBGEcom only for Moroccan businesses?**
A: While we're based in Morocco, OBGEcom works with businesses worldwide. Our platform supports international carriers and multiple currencies.

**Q: Do I need technical skills to use OBGEcom?**
A: No technical skills needed! Our platform is designed for business owners. The setup is guided and most integrations are one-click.

**Q: Can I use OBGEcom with multiple stores?**
A: Yes! All plans (including paid ones) support multiple store connections. The Starter Pack allows one store connection.

### Technical Questions
**Q: What happens if the API goes down?**
A: We have 99.9% uptime SLA. If issues occur, orders are queued and processed automatically when service resumes. Email notifications are sent for any service disruptions.

**Q: Can I customize the customer notifications?**
A: Yes! All email and SMS notifications are fully customizable with your branding and messaging.

**Q: Does OBGEcom support webhooks?**
A: Yes, we provide webhooks for order status updates, delivery tracking, and other events.

### Billing Questions
**Q: When am I billed for paid plans?**
A: Monthly billing, prorated for mid-month upgrades. No annual commitments required.

**Q: Can I downgrade my plan?**
A: Yes, you can downgrade at any time. Changes take effect at the next billing cycle.

**Q: Are there setup fees?**
A: No setup fees for any plan. The Starter Pack is completely free.

## TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

### Common Issues & Solutions
**Issue**: "My orders aren't importing"
**Solution**: Check API credentials and re-authenticate connection. Ensure your store has the latest OBGEcom app installed.

**Issue**: "Carrier assignments are incorrect"
**Solution**: Review your assignment rules in Settings > Automation Rules. Test with sample orders.

**Issue**: "Notifications not sending"
**Solution**: Verify email/SMS settings. Check spam folder. Test with the "Send Test" button.

**Issue**: "Dashboard showing incorrect counts"
**Solution**: Click "Sync Now" in dashboard. Wait 2 minutes for data refresh.
